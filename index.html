A quick checklist

Before you click buy.

01 / SELLER

Check who is selling it.

On marketplaces, the platform name does not always mean the platform is the seller. Look for the brand store or a known retailer.

02 / PRICE

Compare the full cost.

Include shipping, tax, return costs and warranty. A low sticker price is not always the best deal.

03 / REVIEWS

Read recent reviews.

Look for patterns in detailed reviews, not just the star average. Check the exact model and size.

04 / FIT

Confirm compatibility.

Check device compatibility, dimensions, included cables and return policy before adding a product to the final list.
