01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Not just making
websites.

A look at the thinking, curiosity, and creative energy behind Moonflower.

“The internet is more interesting when you make something your own.”

Moonflower is the creative identity behind a growing collection of personal projects. It brings together experiments in web development, interface design, digital entertainment, and small ideas that turn into much bigger builds.

The process is hands-on: imagine a concept, build a working version, test what feels right, fix what does not, and keep improving it. The goal is not to make everything look complicated. It is to make the details feel intentional.

This space documents that process — the projects, the experiments, the lessons, and the direction things are heading.

03 / HOW THE WORK GETS MADE

Thoughtful by
design.

A good experience is more than a nice first impression. It should make sense, respond well, and work across screens.

01 / CLARITY

Make it make sense.

Navigation, structure, and interactions should feel natural. The interface should help people find their way instead of getting in their way.

02 / CHARACTER

Give it a point of view.

Typography, motion, color, and small details work together to make a project recognizable without making it noisy.

03 / ITERATION

Keep making it better.

Build, test, notice the rough edges, and refine. A project can be a work in progress and still be made with care.
